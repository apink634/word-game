How to play
  • Read the clue and guess the 5-letter word. You know its first letter, and you get {{ tries }} guesses per word.
  • Solve it and the chain grows: the next word starts with the last letter of this one (for example STAMP → PRISM → MAGIC).
  • Need help? The hint shows the last letter, so you can see where the chain goes next, but it costs a guess.
  • Miss a word {{ tries }} times and the chain ends. Your score is how long your chain got.
